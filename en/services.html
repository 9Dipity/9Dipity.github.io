    Custom Software

    Tools built for how your business actually works.

    Not how generic software assumes it works.

    Off-the-shelf software is either too rigid, too expensive, or does 90% of what you need while missing the 10% that matters most. When you need something that fits your specific workflow — not a close approximation — custom software is the answer. Built on a proper database, it means your tools grow with the business rather than constraining it.

    What we typically build

    • Inventory & stock management — unified across physical and online stores, with real-time sync
    • Booking & appointment systems — for clinics, service businesses, studios, and hospitality
    • Customer databases (CRM-lite) — purchase history, loyalty programmes, communication logs
    • Order management — from quote through fulfilment, with supplier tracking
    • Pricing & margin tools — supplier price import automation, margin calculators, competitive tracking
    • Internal reporting dashboards — live KPIs, sales by location/channel, staff performance
    • Process automation — eliminating repetitive manual work from your operations
    • Compliance & reporting engines — automated data processing with full audit trails

    Technology — solid, maintainable, yours

    All custom software is built with C#/.NET, SQL Server, and a clean web front-end. Proper dependency injection, test coverage as part of the process — not as an afterthought. You get the source code. If you ever want to take it to another developer, you can.

    • C# / ASP.NET Core backend with REST APIs
    • SQL Server with Dapper or EF Core
    • Modern HTML/CSS/JS front-end (no heavy frameworks)
    • Unit tests — written alongside the code
    • Docker-ready, Azure DevOps CI/CD available

    How it's priced

    Custom software can't have a menu price. The right solution depends entirely on what your business does, how many users you have, what systems already exist, and where the complexity lies. A booking system for a one-person studio is a very different project from inventory software syncing three locations and an online store.

    What to expect: you describe your situation, we ask the right questions, and we come back with a clear scope document and a fixed price before any work starts. No hourly billing. No open-ended engagements. Most projects are quoted within 2–3 business days of first contact.
